Venue Packages

Wedding Packages

All packages include access to The Marlborough House setting at 9 Ling Street in Marlborough, New Hampshire. Catering is available for an additional fee; see the menu sections that follow.

Package 1

Do-It-Yourself Event

$2,500

A single-day venue rental of the hall and ground floor of the house, available from 9:00am to 12:00 midnight.

  • First floor available for the wedding party to get ready and assemble.
  • Ceremony setup includes 100 white folding chairs and white drape to define the ceremony area.
  • Cocktail hour may use three first-floor rooms and the seasonal porch of the main house.
  • Reception inventory includes 100 white folding chairs, round tables, buffet tables, linens, and napkins.
  • Lighting package with tree-branch ceiling designs.
  • House sound system in the main hall available for DJ.
  • Cash bar service includes one bartender and disposable glassware.
Package 2

Setup & Event Support

$3,500

Includes all elements of Package 1, plus staff labor for event setup, room changeover, and cleanup.

  • Labor to set up chairs in the venue prior to the ceremony.
  • Labor to set up and take down chairs and tables for cocktail reception in the house.
  • Labor to change over tables and chairs from ceremony to dinner reception, including dressing tables.
  • Post-event cleanup labor.
  • TMH staff setup for ceremony, cocktail hour, and reception, including placement of tables, chairs, linens, centerpieces, and table settings.
Package 3

Planning & Tableware

$5,000

Includes all elements of Packages 1 and 2, plus event-planning support and tableware.

  • Event planner support for day-of planning and ordering.
  • Help arranging flowers, flatware, caterer, DJ, florist, limousine, accommodations, and delivery/drop-off times.
  • Client remains responsible for final orders and invoices.
  • Tableware includes dinner, salad, appetizer, and dessert plates.
  • Flatware includes knife, dinner fork, salad fork, and spoon.
  • Water glasses included.
Package 1 is a rental-fee-only option. No event labor is included; the client is responsible for setup, cleanup, and changing the room from ceremony to reception. Please return the space in the condition in which it was found.
