Design

Ceremony & Reception

Traditional proscenium ceremony at the front of Main Hall, seated reception at ten round tables, and flexible buffet or lazy susan dinner service.

Ceremony — Primary

Traditional Proscenium

  • Ceremony staged at the front of Main Hall — altar, arch, or draped backdrop facing the guest seating.
  • 100 white folding chairs with white chair covers (Package 3) in rows with a defined center aisle.
  • White drape and expanded lighting package to frame the ceremony zone.
  • House sound available for ceremony music and later DJ/band.
Ceremony — Alternative

In-the-Round / Presidium

  • Optional layout — couple at the center of Main Hall with guests seated around them.
  • Defined aisle for processional; same chair count as proscenium layout.
  • Available if preferred; room flip to round-table reception included either way.
