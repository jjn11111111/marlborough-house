Design

Ceremony & Reception

Traditional proscenium ceremony at the front of Main Hall, seated reception at ten round tables, and flexible buffet or lazy susan dinner service.

Ceremony — Primary

Traditional Proscenium

  • Ceremony staged at the front of Main Hall — altar, arch, or draped backdrop facing the guest seating.
  • 100 white folding chairs with white chair covers (Package 3) in rows with a defined center aisle.
  • White drape and expanded lighting package to frame the ceremony zone.
  • House sound available for ceremony music and later DJ/band.
Ceremony — Alternative

In-the-Round / Presidium

  • Optional layout — couple at the center of Main Hall with guests seated around them.
  • Defined aisle for processional; same chair count as proscenium layout.
  • Available if preferred; room flip to round-table reception included either way.

Reception

10 Round Tables — 100 Guests

  • 10 × 60" round tables — 10 guests per table
  • Mismatched china place settings — vintage floral patterns mixed across each table for an eclectic, collected look
  • Linens, centerpieces, and glassware on every table
  • Dance floor opens after room flip from ceremony to reception
  • Buffet service and/or lazy susan side presentations at table centers

Cocktail Hour — Main House

  • Appetizer selections — passed bites and hors d'oeuvres
  • Spiked punch or lemonade — self-serve station
  • Non-alcoholic options included at the self-serve station

Reception — Dinner & Dessert

  • Buffet service and/or lazy susan side presentations at the center of each round table
  • Open-ended menu — seasonal entrées, accompaniments, and sides; finalized at tasting
  • Dessert — cake provided by client; TMH provides display & service
  • Coffee & tea — served with dessert

Full Cash Bar — Reception

  • Cash bar during reception only — TMH provides bar setup, staffing, and glassware
  • Two bartenders recommended for 100 guests
  • TMH provides bar setup, staffing, and glassware during reception
