Ceremony & Reception

A fresh, youthful layout that puts the couple at the heart of the room and keeps guests close throughout the evening.

Ceremony

In-the-Round / Presidium

  • Ceremony staged at the center of Main Hall where the dance floor will later open.
  • 110 white folding chairs with white chair covers (Package 3) arranged around the couple.
  • Defined aisle for processional; bridal party of 6 bridesmaids and 6 groomsmen.
  • White drape and expanded lighting package to frame the ceremony zone.
  • House sound available for ceremony music and later DJ/band.

Reception Seating — 110 Guests

After cocktail hour, Main Hall resets with the central dance floor open where the ceremony took place. Guest seating is arranged in vignettes surrounding the floor; action stations are placed throughout the room.

  • Central dance floor — open center of Main Hall (former ceremony area)
  • Round table vignettes (4 sections · 10 guests each) — 60" round tables, linens, place settings, centerpieces
  • High communal vignettes (2 sections · 10 guests each) — high communal tables with bar-height seating
  • Mixed lounge vignettes (5 sections · 10 guests each) — loveseats, one-off chairs, ottomans, occasional tables; includes stage placement
  • Action stations — pasta station, salad station, mashed potato bar (positioned around the room)

Cocktail Hour — Main House

  • Charcuterie and artisan cheese boards
  • Cucumber water
  • Iced tea

Reception — Action Stations
