Design

Ceremony & Reception

Two ceremony formats to choose from, a formal banquet-and-round reception layout, and table-side service with a sweetheart table for the grooms on stage.

Ceremony — Option A

Traditional Proscenium

  • Ceremony staged at the front of Main Hall — altar, arch, or draped backdrop in midnight blue with gold and metallic accents.
  • 85 white folding chairs (Package 3) in rows with a defined center aisle.
  • Expanded lighting package to frame the ceremony zone — formal, dramatic, Tom Ford–inspired presentation.
  • House sound available for ceremony music and later DJ/band.
  • Stage area converts to sweetheart table for the grooms during reception.
Ceremony — Option B

In-the-Round / Presidium

  • Grooms at the center of Main Hall with guests seated around them on all sides.
  • Defined aisle for processional; same 85-chair count as proscenium layout.
  • Equally formal staging — midnight blue drape, gold accents, and mixed metallics throughout.
  • Room flip to banquet-and-round reception included either way.

Reception Layout

Banquet & Round Tables — 85 Guests

  • 2 × 24' banquet tables — grounding either long side of Main Hall
  • 4 × 60" round tables — completing guest seating across the floor
  • Sweetheart table for the grooms — positioned on stage (proscenium area)
  • Midnight blue linens with gold and mixed metallic accents at each table
  • Centerpieces — banquet tables: a river treatment of candles running the length of each 24' table — glass-encased, floating, and votive candles with metallic touches and sculptural forest elements
  • Centerpieces — round tables: a central grouping of the same candle presentation — glass-encased, floating, and votive candles with metallic and organic accents
  • Client-provided etched glass coat of arms at each guest place setting
  • Dance floor opens after room flip from ceremony to reception
Visual Direction

Midnight Blue & Gold

  • Palette: midnight blue foundation with gold and mixed metallic accents (brass, silver, pewter)
  • Tom Ford ethos — sleek, tailored, quite formal; restrained luxury over abundance
  • White folding chairs, candlelit centerpieces, and polished table settings
  • Banquet tables — river treatment of candlelight flowing the length of each table
  • Round tables — grouped candle centerpiece at the center of each 60" table
  • Collections of candlelight, organic forest elements, and metallic pops — glass-encased, floating, and votive candles alongside sculptural natural forms for a magical, masculine atmosphere
  • Event-wide styling coordinated to the palette throughout ceremony, cocktail hour, and reception
